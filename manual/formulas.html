Formulas and units

Every number box in the panel accepts maths, units and grid names.

Names you can use

NameMeansWhere
gGrid spacing from LayoutTransform, Arrange gap and nudge
colWidth of the first Layout columnTransform, Arrange
rowHeight of one Layout rowTransform, Arrange
gapGap between columnsTransform, Arrange
vgapGap between rowsTransform, Arrange
w, hThe shape's current width and height, worked out per shapeTransform only
TipLayout's own boxes (spacing, area, left, top) accept maths and units but not grid names, since they define the grid.

Operators

WriteMeans
+ - * /Add, subtract, multiply, divide
( )Brackets, worked out first
2colA number before a name multiplies it, the same as 2*col
*2 in TransformStarting with *, / or + uses the current size: *2 means w*2 in Width

Spaces are ignored. Normal maths order applies: multiply and divide before add and subtract.

Units

WriteMeans
2 or 2in or 2"Inches
30ptPoints, 72 to an inch
1.5cmCentimetres
12mmMillimetres

Boxes keep five decimal places, so 30pt becomes 0.41667, precise enough that 30 pt steps don't drift across a slide.

Common mistakes

  • A name that doesn't exist, such as column, can't be worked out. Use col.
  • Unbalanced brackets.
  • Dividing by zero.
  • w and h outside Transform. They only mean something for a shape being resized.
  • Typing 0.42 when your guides are 30 pt. Use 30pt instead for an exact match.
