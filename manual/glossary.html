Glossary

The terms used across the panel and this manual.

Terms

Grid area
The part of the slide your content sits in, set in Layout by left, top, width and height.
g (grid spacing)
The basic step of the whole panel. Gaps, snapping and table sizes are measured in g.
Gap fraction
Layout's gaps are a fraction of g: 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4 or 1.
Detail level
Basic, More or Full. Each tool shows more options at higher levels.
Sample
Read formatting from the selection into a new style.
Linked
Text, a shape or a table that follows a style. Update all changes linked items.
Local change
A setting you changed by hand on a linked item. Shown with a + and kept by Update all.
Lookalike
Text or a shape that matches a style but isn't linked, often after recolouring from the ribbon.
Update all
Pushes style edits to every linked item on normal slides, after showing what will change.
Masters and layouts
The template slides behind your slides. Slide Tools never changes them.
Presentation guides
Guides you can drag on any slide. Templates usually bring them.
Template guides
Guides set in the slide master or layout, locked outside Slide Master view.
Category column
A table column of stacked labels, such as a year, shown once per group.
Label column
A table column of row names that don't repeat.
Data column
A table column of numbers, right aligned.
Total row
A table row whose first filled cell starts with Total.
Line check
A one-time test that finds out how your PowerPoint shows table lines.
Theme colours
The ten colours of the presentation's theme, with tints and shades.
Template colours
Custom colours saved in the template's theme.
Your colours
A palette you build, saved in the presentation.
