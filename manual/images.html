Images

Put pictures into shapes and adjust their crop.

Overview

Images puts pictures into shapes the way a picture placeholder does, and moves or resets the crop of pictures. Because the whole picture is kept, PowerPoint's own Crop button can pan and zoom it afterwards.

Quick start

  1. Draw and style a shape, such as a rounded card.
  2. Select it, copy a picture, click the paste box and press Ctrl+V.
  3. Click a crop position to choose what shows, or use PowerPoint's Crop button to fine tune it.

How it works

PowerPoint add-ins can't place a cropped picture directly, so the slide is exported, the picture is added to that copy in memory, and the copy replaces the slide. Nothing is sent anywhere, and only the slide itself changes, never the master or layouts. The picture keeps all its pixels, and the crop is stored the same way PowerPoint stores its own crops.

The crop fills the shape without stretching. A wide picture in a square shape loses some of its sides, and the crop position decides which part stays: centre keeps the middle, top middle keeps the top, and so on.

TipSlide comments may not carry over to the replaced slide, and Undo takes more than one step. Images asks first, like Size and Copies do, unless you chose Don't ask again.

Recipes

A photo card

A rounded card that holds a photo, ready to pan and swap.

  1. Draw the card with the corners and outline you want.
  2. Set the crop position to where the subject is, such as top middle for a portrait.
  3. Select the card, click the paste box and press Ctrl+V.
  4. To swap the photo later, select it and paste a new one. The frame and crop position stay.

Fill a row of picture frames

Several shapes filled with the same picture in one step, for a quick mock up.

  1. Select all the shapes.
  2. Paste the picture once. Each shape gets its own crop to fit its proportions.

Line up faces across photos

Portraits cropped the same way.

  1. Select the pictures.
  2. Click top middle in Crop position.

Limits and edge cases

  • Pictures can't hold text, so text in a shape is removed when a picture is placed in it. The panel says so.
  • Groups, tables, charts and lines can't hold a picture.
  • Rotated and flipped shapes keep their rotation and flip.
  • This needs PowerPoint API 1.8.
