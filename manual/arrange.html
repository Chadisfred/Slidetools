Position

Line up, space, nudge and snap shapes, and copy position and size between shapes.

Overview

Position lines shapes up, spaces them with exact gaps, nudges them by the grid step, snaps them to the grid or your template's guides, and copies one shape's position and size onto others.

Quick start

  1. Select shapes.
  2. Click an align button, or Across or Down to space them.

How it works

Distribute versus even gaps

  • Across and Down keep the first shape still and place each next shape exactly the gap after the previous one. The row may get longer or shorter.
  • Even gaps keeps both outer shapes still and works out the gap that fits. The row keeps its length.

Snap

Snap here is the same as in Grid: Guides, Columns or Steps, with the same options. The two share their settings, so changing the mode in one changes it in the other.

Copy and paste

Paste lines up the chosen point. For example, with Paste from set to centre and Position only, each shape is moved so its centre sits on the copied shape's centre, keeping its own size.

Recipes

Space a row of icons exactly 1g apart

Icons with identical gaps.

  1. Select the icons.
  2. Leave Gap between shapes as g.
  3. Click Across.

Centre a block on the grid area

A group of shapes centred in the content area.

  1. Select the shapes.
  2. At More, set Align to Grid area.
  3. Click Align centres, then Align middles.

Put a logo in the same spot on every slide

Exact same position on each slide.

  1. Select the logo where it should be and click Copy.
  2. Go to another slide, select its logo, choose Position, and click Paste.

Make a title box the same size everywhere

Same size, different positions.

  1. Copy the reference title.
  2. On each other slide, select the title, choose Size, and set Paste from to the point that should stay still.
  3. Click Paste.

Limits and edge cases

  • Align and distribute work on the shapes you select. Shapes inside a group move with the group.
  • Swap needs exactly two shapes. Even gaps needs three or more.
  • The copied position is forgotten when the panel closes.
