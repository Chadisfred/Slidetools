Arrange

Line up, space and snap shapes onto your grid or your template's guides.

Overview

Arrange lines shapes up, spaces them with exact gaps, and snaps them onto your template's guides or the Layout grid.

Quick start

  1. Select shapes.
  2. Click an align button, Across or Down to space them, or Snap.

How it works

Distribute versus even gaps

  • Across and Down keep the first shape still and place each next shape exactly the gap after the previous one. The row may get longer or shorter.
  • Even gaps keeps both outer shapes still and works out the gap that fits. The row keeps its length.

Snap rules

  • Snap always moves the top left corner to the nearest line, both across and down.
  • Columns: the lines are each column's left edge and each row's top edge. With Resize to the cells it covers, the right and bottom edges move to the nearest column and row ends.
  • Steps: the lines are every g from the grid area's top left, in both directions.
  • Guides: the lines are the guides themselves. Vertical guides for left and right, horizontal guides for top and bottom.

Where guides come from

PowerPoint keeps guides in two places: presentation guides, which you can drag on any slide (templates usually bring these), and template guides in the slide master or layout, which are locked outside Slide Master view. Arrange reads a copy of the open file, so it finds both. Nothing in the file is changed.

The guide report

Guide positions are stored to the nearest eighth of a point. The report finds the most common spacing, then lists any guide sitting a little off that pattern (within a point or 10% of the spacing) and by how much. Guides far off the pattern, such as a centre line, are treated as deliberate.

Why shapes drift off guidesPowerPoint shows guide positions rounded to two decimals, so a 30 pt grid (0.41667 in) looks like 0.42. If you type 0.42 as g, shapes drift further from the guides across the slide. Use in Layout copies the exact value.

Recipes

Fix shapes drifting off the guides

Shapes that look slightly off the template guides, more so on the right.

  1. Choose Guides in Arrange.
  2. Switch Arrange to More and read the guide report. Note any guide marked slightly off.
  3. Click Use in Layout so g matches the guides exactly.
  4. Select the shapes and click Snap.

Space a row of icons exactly 1g apart

Icons with identical gaps.

  1. Select the icons.
  2. Leave Gap between shapes as g.
  3. Click Across.

Centre a block on the grid area

A group of shapes centred in the content area.

  1. Select the shapes.
  2. At More, set Align to Grid area.
  3. Click Align centres, then Align middles.

Snap a whole slide to the template

Every shape on the slide sitting on a guide.

  1. Select all with Ctrl+A.
  2. Choose Guides, and at More turn on Snap all four edges for exact widths.
  3. Click Snap.

Limits and edge cases

  • Snap and align work on the shapes you select. Shapes inside a group move with the group.
  • Reading guides from the whole file needs the file to be available to add-ins. If it isn't, the panel falls back to a copy of the slide, which only has template guides, and the report says so.
  • Swap needs exactly two shapes. Even gaps needs three or more.
  • Reading the slide copy needs PowerPoint API 1.8.
